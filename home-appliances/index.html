Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: November 3, 2026
Developer responsible for the app: Andrei Tatarenko
Android package: com.at.kidscoloring.kids_coloring_home_appliances

This policy explains how the Android app handles information during coloring, personalized stories and the adventure with Pip. It also covers this policy website and privacy-related correspondence.

Privacy contact: tatarenkoandrei7@gmail.com. Parents and guardians can use this address for questions and data requests.

Photos are processed on your device. The app does not upload camera photos or extracted portraits to the developer, Firebase, advertising services or a cloud image-processing service.

Technical data can leave the device. Google services used for analytics, crash reporting, advertising and ML Kit diagnostics can process usage information and technical identifiers. This can happen even if you do not use the camera.

No account is required. Drawings, story and adventure progress, and preferences are stored locally. Camera permission is optional for ordinary coloring and the adventure.

1. Camera photos and the story portrait

The optional personalization feature opens the camera after Android grants permission. It uses a photo to create a cutout portrait for the story character. The camera does not record sound, and the app does not request microphone permission.

Google ML Kit face detection and selfie segmentation run on the device to locate the face, check the photo and remove the background. Face positions, contours and segmentation masks are used for this processing; they are not kept as an identity database. The feature does not identify a person, authenticate them, or compare them with other people. We do not use these photos or portraits to train AI models.

The app keeps the current cutout in private app storage until you replace or delete it. This portrait directory is excluded from Android app backup. Saved story thumbnails exclude the face; the app displays the current portrait over them when needed.

The temporary camera file is removed after its bytes are read. Temporary processing files are removed when processing finishes, including handled failures. If the app is interrupted, remaining temporary files are cleaned up when the relevant app or portrait feature next starts. The original photo can remain temporarily in memory while you review or process it.

Google ML Kit can still send operational metrics described in section 3. Those metrics are separate from the photo and portrait contents. See ML Kit’s privacy information.

2. Drawings, progress and settings

The app stores coloring choices and strokes, saved drawings and story thumbnails, completed story pages, adventure checkpoints and unlocked rooms, and preferences such as language, character choice, play mode and voice settings. This lets you continue activities and keep your selections after closing the app.

These saves are stored on the device. The app does not offer a developer-hosted account, cloud gallery, public profile, chat or social sharing service. Drawing contents and the saved adventure state are not included in the app’s custom analytics events.

Android or your device manufacturer may back up and restore local app data other than the excluded portrait directory, depending on your device and backup settings. Such backups are controlled by that service, not by the developer.

3. Analytics, diagnostics and advertising

Firebase Analytics

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature use. Custom events include opening screens, categories, stories and the gallery, selecting a coloring mode, and viewing a coloring page. They can include a built-in picture name or identifier, a category, story identifier or chapter number. These are app content labels, not a child’s name or the contents of their drawing.

The SDK also processes technical and usage data, including an app-instance identifier, app and device characteristics, operating-system version, language, sessions and interactions. Google may derive approximate location, such as a country or city, from a network IP address. The app does not request precise device location.

Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics receives error reports to help diagnose failures. Reports can contain installation or session identifiers, device and app details, crash timestamps, exception messages and stack traces. The app also supplies the last built-in picture identifier, coloring mode, story identifier and chapter number. It does not attach photos, portraits, drawings or screenshots to crash reports. See Firebase’s privacy and security information.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

In addition to processing images locally, ML Kit can send Google device and app information, per-installation identifiers, API configuration, input/output sizes, performance measurements and error or feature-use events. It may contact Google for models, updates or compatibility information. See ML Kit’s Android data disclosures.

Google AdMob and privacy-status checks

The app includes Google AdMob for interstitial advertising at activity transitions. Ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment, general-audience content and non-personalized advertising. The app requests an under-age-of-consent privacy status through Google’s User Messaging Platform and requests ads only when that service permits it. Ads may be unavailable depending on connectivity, the service response or the build’s configuration.

Non-personalized advertising still involves technical data. Google’s advertising services may process an IP address and approximate location derived from it, app and device information, ad interactions, diagnostic data and permitted app/device identifiers, for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. We do not provide portraits, photos, drawing contents or a child’s name to these services.

The app disables Firebase advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalization signals and removes the Android advertising ID permission. These settings do not disable all analytics or technical identifiers. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosures and Google’s Privacy Policy.

There is currently no in-app switch to disable Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Turning off camera permission or voice playback does not disable those services.

4. Read-aloud and voice features

The app sends its built-in story text and game hints to the Android text-to-speech engine selected on your device. It does not listen to or record the user’s voice. Depending on the engine and selected voice, speech may be generated locally or involve that provider’s online service. Its own settings and privacy policy apply. Use the app’s playback controls and device text-to-speech settings to manage this feature.

5. Children and parental supervision

The app offers activities for children. A parent or guardian should supervise use of the optional photo feature and manage device permissions. We do not require a child to provide a name, date of birth, email address, phone number or account credentials to play. The app does not request access to contacts, precise location, the microphone or shared external storage.

The technical data processing in section 3 can occur while a child uses the app. We do not sell personal information or use children’s portraits for advertising, identification or behavioral profiling. Advertising requests are configured as described above.

Parents or guardians can contact us to ask about a child’s information or request action concerning information we can identify. Please contact us as an adult and do not send a child’s photo, passwords or sensitive documents. A privacy policy is not a substitute for any separate notice or consent required by applicable law.

6. Who receives information

Google provides the Firebase, ML Kit, AdMob and privacy-status services described above. The device’s speech and backup providers may process information when their services are used. GitHub hosts this policy website. The developer can access the usage reports and crash diagnostics made available through the configured Google services, but does not receive locally stored portraits or drawings through the app.

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Email providers process that correspondence to deliver and store it. Information may also need to be disclosed when required by law or to address a security incident, limited to what is necessary.

Providers may process technical data and correspondence in countries other than yours. Their applicable privacy policies and data-protection terms describe their processing and international-transfer safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Current portrait: retained locally until replaced or deleted through the app menu, or removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling. Revoking camera permission alone does not delete it.
  • Other local saves: retained until replaced or removed by available app controls, clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling. Deleting the portrait does not erase drawings or game progress. Operating-system backups can have a separate lifetime and can restore eligible saves; manage those copies in your backup settings.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics retention depends on the project’s retention settings and data type. Aggregated reports may be kept longer than individual event data. Contact us for questions about retention or deletion of data associated with this app.
  • Crash reports: Google documents a 90-day retention period for Crashlytics crash data and associated identifiers before removal from its live and backup systems begins.
  • Other provider data: advertising, ML Kit, speech, email and website-hosting data follow the relevant service’s retention rules and applicable requirements.
  • Support messages: kept as needed to resolve the request, follow up on the issue, and meet applicable legal or security obligations.

Clearing or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete technical reports already sent to Google or messages sent by email. Contact us for a request concerning those records. Because no app account identifies you, we may need limited additional information to find relevant records and may not be able to link an installation or report to a particular person. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a portrait that exists only on your device.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can revoke camera permission in Android Settings, delete or replace the portrait in the app menu, use available controls to manage drawings and progress, and clear local app storage in Android Settings. Device menus differ by manufacturer. Clearing storage removes local saves and preferences.

Depending on applicable law, you or a parent acting for a child may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also be entitled to complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact tatarenkoandrei7@gmail.com, identify this app and explain your request. We may ask for proportionate information to verify authority and locate the data, without requesting more information than needed.

9. Security

The app uses private Android storage for its files and the no-backup directory for the current portrait. Google’s SDK services use encrypted network connections. Protecting the device, access to backups and access to your email also matters. No method of storage or transmission provides an absolute security guarantee.
