Little Coloring: Animals

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026
Developer: Andrei Tatarenko

This policy explains how Little Coloring: Animals (the “app”) handles information. It covers coloring activities, personalized stories, saved drawings, and the Android app’s analytics and crash reporting.

Contact: tatarenkoandrei7@gmail.com. Parents and guardians may use this address for privacy questions or deletion requests.

1. Photos and personalized stories

The optional story personalization feature uses the camera, with your permission, to create a portrait for a story character. Photo processing, including face detection and background removal with Google ML Kit, takes place on your device. The app does not upload your photo or extracted portrait to the developer or send it as an analytics event. Face detection is used to position and crop the portrait, not to identify a person.

The app retains the extracted portrait in private local storage so it can be reused in stories. Temporary processing photos are deleted after processing; interrupted temporary files are cleaned up when the feature is loaded again. You can replace or delete the portrait in the app menu. The Android portrait storage is excluded from Android app backup. Camera capture does not record audio.

2. Drawings and settings

Coloring progress, saved drawings, story progress, and settings such as language and character choice are stored locally on your device. They are used to restore your work and preferences. The app does not operate a developer-hosted account or cloud drawing service. Operating-system backup settings may apply to local app data other than the excluded portrait storage.

3. Analytics and crash reports

The Android app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features work well. Custom events describe selected coloring modes and categories, opening stories or the gallery, and viewing a coloring canvas. Canvas events may include a picture identifier and name, story identifier, and chapter number. These events do not include the portrait or drawing contents.

Google’s SDKs also process technical information, such as app instance identifiers, app and operating-system information, and usage information. Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports and diagnostics, including installation identifiers, device information, stack traces, and the last picture, coloring mode, story, or chapter identifiers, to help investigate failures.

The Android app disables advertising ID collection and advertising personalization signals. It does not include advertising. Analytics and crash reporting currently have no in-app on/off control.

4. Service providers

Google provides Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and ML Kit. Although ML Kit processes images on-device, its SDK can send operational and usage metrics to Google. These providers may process technical data on servers outside your country, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards.

  • Firebase privacy and security information
  • ML Kit terms and data processing information

We do not sell personal information or use portraits for advertising. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use them to respond to your request.

5. Children and parents

The app offers activities for children. Parents and guardians should supervise use of the optional camera feature and manage device permissions. No name, email address, or account registration is required to color or use stories. Technical data collection described above may occur when a child uses the app. Please contact us if you have questions about a child’s information; do not send a child’s photo or other sensitive information by email unless necessary to resolve your request.

6. Retention and deletion

The current portrait remains on the device until replaced, deleted from the app menu, or removed by clearing app data or uninstalling. Local progress and settings remain until removed using available app controls, clearing app data, or uninstalling; device backups may have their own retention and restoration settings.

Firebase and Google Analytics retain technical data according to the relevant service and project retention settings. Crashlytics retains crash data for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Deleting local app data does not itself delete reports already sent to Google. Contact us for questions or requests concerning access, correction, or deletion, where applicable. We may need additional non-sensitive details to locate a report, and may not be able to associate a report with a particular person.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

7. Security and your choices

Portraits are kept in the app’s private device storage, and Google services use encrypted network connections. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. You can revoke camera permission in device settings, delete the stored portrait in the app menu, or clear local app data in device settings. Revoking camera permission does not by itself delete an existing portrait.

8. This policy website and updates

This page does not use analytics scripts or cookies added by the developer. GitHub Pages hosts the page and may process visitors’ technical information to operate and secure its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
